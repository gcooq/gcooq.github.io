Open-World Learning

How can agents adapt to distribution shifts and new tasks while retaining useful knowledge over time?

Reasoning & Action

From research foundations to agents

Existing foundations. My work spans trajectory understanding, dynamic graph learning, open-world anomaly detection, and continual learning.

Future direction. I aim to connect these foundations in spatio-temporal agents with memory, interaction, and decision-making capabilities, using open-world learning to support long-term adaptation.

Shared learning foundations

Recognizing the unknown

Learning to Discover Anomalous Spatiotemporal Trajectory via Open-World State Space Model

Knowledge-Based Systems · 2025

OSSL combines a spatio-temporal state-space block with interacting adapters and open-world objectives to classify known trajectory behaviors and discover multiple unknown anomalous patterns.

Qiang Gao, Chaoran Liu, Li Huang*, Goce Trajcevski, Qing Guo, Fan Zhou.

Paper ↗

Transferring knowledge across tasks

Enhancing Knowledge Transfer for Task Incremental Learning with Data-free Subnetwork

NeurIPS · 2023

DSN combines neuron-level masks with replay generated without past training data, enabling knowledge transfer to new tasks and back to earlier tasks while mitigating forgetting.

Qiang Gao#, Xiaojun Shan#, Yuchen Zhang, Fan Zhou*.

Paper ↗

Geospatial Intelligence 地理空间智能

Selected work on learning and modeling in changing geographic environments.

Continual spatio-temporal learning

Enhancing Fine-Grained Urban Flow Inference via Incremental Neural Operator

IJCAI · 2024

UNOI treats coarse- and fine-grained urban flows as continuous functions and combines neural operators with flow- and task-incremental learning to address scale sensitivity and catastrophic forgetting.

Qiang Gao, Xiaolong Song, Li Huang*, Goce Trajcevski, Fan Zhou, Xueqin Chen.

Paper ↗

Modeling changing dependencies

Responsive Dynamic Graph Disentanglement for Metro Flow Forecasting

AAAI · 2025

ReDyNet responds to changes in metro flow and external factors when constructing dynamic graphs, then uses information-bottleneck-based disentanglement to reduce redundant spatial signals.

Qiang Gao, Zizheng Wang, Li Huang*, Goce Trajcevski, Guisong Liu, Xueqin Chen*.

Paper ↗

Financial Spatial Intelligence 金融空间智能

Selected work on learning evolving relationships in financial systems under uncertainty.

Relational modeling & uncertainty

Relational Stock Selection via Probabilistic State Space Learning

IEEE Transactions on Knowledge and Data Engineering · 2025

RSSL models higher-order stock relationships with a tree structure and attention, then uses a probabilistic Kalman network to track changing stock dynamics with uncertainty estimates.

Qiang Gao, Zhengxiang Liu, Li Huang*, Kunpeng Zhang, Jun Wang, Guisong Liu.

Paper ↗

Risk-aware dependency learning

Progressive Dependency Representation Learning for Stock Ranking in Uncertain Risk Contrasting

KDD · 2025

PDU uses guidance from two environments to progressively model dependencies in historical trading signals while accounting for uncertain risk in stock ranking.

Li Huang, Yanzhe Xie, Qiang Gao*, Kunpeng Zhang, Guisong Liu, Xueqin Chen.

Chengdu Spatio-Temporal Visualization

Interactive map of Chengdu with 6 types of spatio-temporal data: taxi trajectories, POIs, metro stations, check-in data, weather dynamics, and flow lines — all animated over time on an AMap base layer.

Illustrative simulation · Generated data inspired by T-Drive, Geolife, and Brightkite patterns; not a replay of original datasets.
